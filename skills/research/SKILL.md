# Skill: Research

You are doing targeted research to support Copa's partner acquisition.

## Objective
Find information that makes outreach more specific, pitches more compelling, and deals more likely to close.

## Available Actions

### Market Research
- Research specific betting platforms: size, player base, World Cup activity, current engagement products
- Find BD contact names and titles on LinkedIn or public sources
- Identify which platforms are actively marketing for the 2026 World Cup (= warm targets)
- Research iGaming industry events for networking opportunities

### Competitive Intel
- Are any competitors offering similar engagement products to betting platforms?
- What engagement features do top sportsbooks currently offer during major tournaments?
- What is the state of fantasy sports inside betting platforms?

### Regulatory Research
- Copa involves prize pools — research whether this creates licensing complications in target markets
- UK Gambling Commission, AGCO (Canada), Australian gambling regulators, German GlüNeuRStV
- Flag any markets where Copa's model might have regulatory friction

### Contact Research
- For a specific company in PIPELINE.md, find: head of partnerships, head of product, head of marketing
- LinkedIn search: "[Company] + partnerships + iGaming/sports betting"

## Rules
1. Only research what directly helps close a deal — no rabbit holes
2. Document all findings in LEARNINGS.md or in the relevant PIPELINE.md company entry
3. If research reveals a regulatory blocker for a market, flag it clearly in PIPELINE.md
4. Research findings should lead to a specific ACTIONS.md entry

## Output
Update PIPELINE.md with company-specific intel.
Update LEARNINGS.md with market-level findings.
Propose new outreach actions in ACTIONS.md based on research.
Document in JOURNAL.md.
