# Skill: Convert

You are moving warm partner conversations to signed agreements.

## Objective
For partners who are engaged or in demo stage — identify what's blocking them and remove it.

## Available Actions

### Objection Handling
- Review OUTREACH.md for what objections partners have raised
- Draft specific responses to common objections:
  - "We already have a fantasy product" → Copa is retention/engagement, not competing fantasy
  - "Integration is too complex" → Single webhook, we handle everything else
  - "We need to involve legal/compliance" → Draft clean term sheet to accelerate review
  - "What if Copa doesn't get traction?" → Exclusivity means no risk to try; if it flops, no cost to partner
- Propose responses as PENDING in ACTIONS.md

### Deal Acceleration
- If a partner has gone quiet, draft re-engagement message
- If a partner is close but stalling, draft urgency message: "We can only hold [Market] exclusivity until [Date]"
- Always propose in ACTIONS.md as PENDING

### Term Negotiation Support
- Review any term counter-proposals from partners
- Identify acceptable vs. unacceptable variations to the standard offer
- Document negotiation guidance in MONETIZATION.md

### Reference Building
- If Copa has any signed partners, draft brief case study or reference doc
- Warm intros from satisfied partners to prospects in other markets

## Rules
1. Never give up market exclusivity — it's the most valuable thing Copa offers
2. Rev share can flex from 20% to 25% for large platforms or group deals — document any changes in MONETIZATION.md
3. All external communications proposed as PENDING in ACTIONS.md
4. Log every conversation update in OUTREACH.md

## Output
Write draft responses to ACTIONS.md as PENDING.
Update OUTREACH.md with conversation status.
Update MONETIZATION.md with any negotiation learnings.
Log in JOURNAL.md.
