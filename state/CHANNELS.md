## Channel Content & Execution Plan

### 1. WhatsApp / Telegram Groups (**PRIMARY**)
- **Pre-launch (now–June 1):** Drop sample Cards in friendly/test groups, collect social proof, nudge group organizers on “try this with your crew.”
- **Launch week (June 10–18):** Enable first Organizers to seed Group Cards to active chats—track share→waitlist conversion.
- **Tournament:** Daily Card share by Organizers, group leaderboards, meme recaps.
- **Who posts:** Users/Organizers.
- **Content:** Copa Card, Group leaderboard Card.
- **Frequency:** Daily (after each match’s reveal).

### 2. Twitter / X
- **Pre-launch:** Show Card demos, explain new mechanic, DM creators for challenge posts.
- **Launch week:** Public reveal of Cards for first World Cup matches, tag/engage creators, “beat the crowd” meme campaigns.
- **Tournament:** Memes of best/worst gap Reads, daily Card threads, challenge replies.
- **Who posts:** Copa, creators, UGC.
- **Content:** Card PNGs, memeable gaps, group challenge calls.
- **Frequency:** Daily per match, plus real-time meme surges.

### 3. Reddit (r/soccer, national team subs)
- **Pre-launch:** Secure mod approval with meta “would you play?” threads, demo Card.
- **Launch week:** Matchday Card posts in pre-approved threads; crowd vs sub reveal moments.
- **Tournament:** Daily Card/Canon recap, meme comments, AMAs.
- **Who posts:** Copa (with mod OK), UGC.
- **Content:** Crowd consensus, recap moment image.
- **Frequency:** Per high-drama match, daily in group stage.

### 4. Discord (football/gaming servers)
- **Pre-launch:** DM server admins for pilot group, test Group Card.
- **Launch:** Card drop and meme contests (“our group’s gap”).
- **Tournament:** Daily recaps, voice channel plays, meme contests.
- **Who posts:** Copa, admin, UGC.
- **Content:** PNGs, leaderboard embeds.
- **Frequency:** Daily.

### 5. Email Waitlist (owned asset)
- **Pre-launch:** Landing page, email field for notif/launch.
- **Launch:** “Game is live” and Card delivery.
- **Tournament:** Daily Card delivery, streak updates.
- **Who posts:** Copa auto/manual.
- **Content:** Card PNG, streak/FOMO.
- **Frequency:** 1 per reveal per player.

### 6. TikTok / IG Reels
- **Pre-launch:** Short demo videos, duet prompts to microcreators.
- **Launch:** Creator demo challenges.
- **Tournament:** “My group missed by 45%!” memes, Card reveals.
- **Who posts:** Creators, players.
- **Content:** Card reveals, meme duets.
- **Frequency:** 2–3/demo per match week.

### 7. YouTube/Podcasts
- **Pre-launch:** Demo slots on mid-sized channels/pods.
- **Launch:** Group challenge/guest playthroughs.
- **Tournament:** “Can you read the crowd?” featurettes.
- **Who posts:** Copa (guest), creators.
- **Content:** Play demo, reveal reactions.
- **Frequency:** 1–3/week.

### 8. Football Newsletters
- **Pre-launch:** Pitch “crowd moment” recaps as microsegment.
- **Launch/Tournament:** Quick Card meme-inserts or moment recaps.
- **Who posts:** Copa, newsletter authors.
- **Content:** Card snippets, crowd data.
- **Frequency:** Weekly or opt-in periodicals.
