## March 23, 2026 — Strategic Pivot

**Lesson:** The agent was optimizing for the wrong thing. Consumer game design ≠ B2B partnership sales. The Copa product is already designed. The leverage is in closing deals, not refining mechanics.

**Key insight on the pitch:**
- The partner's ROI is primarily *incremental betting volume*, not the 20% revenue share. Revenue share is a bonus on top of the core retention value.
- When pitching, lead with retention (48-day engagement) and bet frequency (spins tied to wins), not the revenue table. The table closes the deal; the retention story opens the door.

**Key insight on pipeline:**
- Flutter Group and Entain together cover ~15+ major markets. A group BD conversation bypasses the need to pitch each brand individually. Prioritize group conversations over brand-by-brand outreach.

**Key insight on urgency:**
- June 11, 2026 is the hard deadline. Any partner signed after May 1 risks not having integration complete by launch. Real urgency is **April 15, 2026** to allow 8 weeks for integration testing.
