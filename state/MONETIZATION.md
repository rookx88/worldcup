# Monetization

## Model

Copa earns revenue through **entry fee revenue sharing** with betting platform partners.

- Players pay **$10 per squad entry** directly through the partner's platform
- **80%** goes to the player prize pool
- **20%** is Copa's revenue share (paid by the partner, or collected directly and remitted)

Multiple entries per player are permitted, multiplying revenue.

---

## Why This Model Works for Partners

1. **Incremental betting volume** — Copa's spin mechanic ties every bet to squad progression, directly increasing bet frequency among Copa players
2. **Retention without cost** — Players return for 48 days across 104 matches, far beyond their original bet
3. **Promotional tool** — Free spin bundles replace generic deposit bonuses with a Copa-branded reward that drives more bets
4. **Zero platform rebuild** — Single webhook integration means negligible engineering cost

## Negotiation Notes

- 20% revenue share is the **default offer** — room to flex up to 25% for large partners or strategic markets
- For Flutter/Entain group deals (multi-market), offer **22%** across all markets as the incentive for group commitment
- Exclusivity per market is firm — don't give it up; it's our best closing lever
- Partnership agreement should include: market exclusivity, revenue share rate, integration timeline, minimum launch date (June 1, 2026 for pre-tournament testing)
